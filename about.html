About

Hi, I'm Abdan.

I'm someone who spends a lot of time thinking, reading, watching films, drinking coffee and wondering where the next road might lead.

I like quiet places, meaningful conversations, photographs that feel imperfect and stories that make ordinary life feel a little less ordinary.

I made this website because the internet moves very quickly.

I wanted somewhere slower.

Somewhere I can put pieces of my life without worrying whether they are useful to anyone.

If you found this place, welcome.
